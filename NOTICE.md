It contains material from [survev](https://github.com/survev/survev) (GPL-3.0):

- `docs/img/` and `docs/audio/`: images and sounds of the survev client, unchanged
- `docs/catalog.json`: data taken from survev's object, map and sound definitions

The scripts in `docs/` (`viewer.js`, `real.js`, `sound.js`, `replay.js`) draw and sound the match the way the survev client does. The files in `docs/replays/` are recordings of matches played on a local survev server.
