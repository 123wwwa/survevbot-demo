What this is

Squads of four agents trained with multi-agent reinforcement learning to play survev, an open-source 2D battle royale. These matches were played on a local server, and nobody in them is human.

The model decides where to go, who to fight and what to use. Scripts carry that out: aiming, pathfinding, healing, throws. More about the project
